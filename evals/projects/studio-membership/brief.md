# Brief — studio-membership

## Pitch

A small yoga studio, Still Point, wants a public page that sells its class
passes, and a simple members' area where members see the week's timetable and
book a spot in a class.

## Answers

- **Problem:** bookings come in by Instagram DM and the owner keeps a paper list; classes over- and under-fill and new visitors can't find prices.
- **Users:** visitors deciding whether to try a class (public page); members booking classes (members' area); the owner who sets the timetable. Members come first when needs conflict.
- **Today's alternative:** Instagram DMs and a paper sign-up sheet at the door.
- **Core flows:**
  1. Visitor reads the public page and finds prices and the intro offer — acceptance: price list and intro offer visible on a phone without hunting.
  2. Member signs in with a magic link — acceptance: link email arrives and signs them in.
  3. Member sees this week's timetable and books a class — acceptance: booking reduces the spots left, and a full class can't be booked.
  4. Member cancels a booking — acceptance: the spot frees immediately.
  5. Owner edits the timetable — acceptance: changes show in the members' timetable.
- **First release:** flows 1–4. Owner editing waits; the owner edits a seed file for now.
- **Riskiest assumptions:** members will book in the app rather than DM.
- **Non-goals:** no payments in the app (passes are sold in person), no waitlist, no native app, single studio.
- **Success:** a month of classes booked in the app with no paper list.
- **Surfaces:** a public marketing page and a members' web app, both mostly on phones.
- **Brand and feel:** no brand. Feel: calm, warm, grounded. Not: gym-aggressive or neon. References they like: Headspace, Aesop. They have their own photographs of the studio.
- **Interaction frequency:** members book a few times a week; visitors come once.
- **Constraints:** one freelance developer fluent in TypeScript and Next.js; a free or near-free host; launch in a month.
- **Scale:** about 150 members; peak is Sunday evening when the week's timetable opens.
- **Size:** tool.
