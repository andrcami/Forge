# Brief — bakery-landing

## Pitch

A neighbourhood sourdough bakery, Ferment & Field, wants a one-page website:
what they bake, opening hours, where they are, and a link to their existing
online pre-order shop. They have a fixed brand already.

## Answers

- **Problem:** people find the bakery on Instagram but have no page with hours, address, and the pre-order link.
- **Users:** locals and passers-by on their phones; the owner who updates the weekly bake list twice a month.
- **Today's alternative:** an Instagram bio and a pinned post.
- **Core flows:**
  1. Find hours and address — acceptance: visible without scrolling on a phone.
  2. See this week's bakes — acceptance: the list with photos renders from a single editable file.
  3. Go to pre-order — acceptance: one tap to the existing shop (external link).
- **First release:** all three.
- **Riskiest assumptions:** the owner can edit one Markdown or JSON file to change the bake list.
- **Non-goals:** no ordering or payments on this site, no CMS, no blog, no accounts, no backend.
- **Success:** Instagram bio link goes to the site; owner updates the bake list without help.
- **Surfaces:** a single public marketing page. Static.
- **Brand and feel:** fixed brand — primary colour #1F3A2E (deep green), accent #E9B44C (wheat), typeface Fraunces for headings, Inter for body. Feel: warm, crafted, unhurried. Not: slick or techy. They have their own photographs of the bread and the shop.
- **Constraints:** budget is free static hosting; built by one freelancer fluent in HTML/CSS and Astro; two weeks.
- **Size:** tool.
