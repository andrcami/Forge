# Code of Conduct

This project follows the spirit of the
[Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

## Our pledge

We want taking part in this project to be a welcoming, harassment-free
experience for everyone, whatever their background, experience or identity.

## What we expect

- Be respectful and patient, including when you disagree.
- Give and accept feedback on the work, not the person.
- Assume good intent, and ask before assuming.
- Help newcomers find their way.

## What is not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, or personal or political attacks
- Sexualised language or imagery
- Publishing anyone's private information without their explicit permission
- Any other conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional setting

## Scope

This applies in all project spaces: issues, pull requests, discussions and
reviews, and wherever someone represents the project.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report a problem to the maintainer, @andrcami, through the contact details on
their GitHub profile. Reports are handled in confidence. The maintainer may
edit or remove comments, commits, issues and pull requests that break this
code, and may block anyone who repeatedly or seriously breaks it.
