Look at the lesson body, the text after the closing `---` of the frontmatter.

PASS if its first sentence is the rule, stated as an instruction that starts with a command verb (for example "Verify…", "Compute…", "Do not…"), in 20 words or fewer, and the evidence (what happened in relay) comes after the rule, not before it.
FAIL if the body opens with the story, a heading, or a description ("The handler…", "We found…", "When…"), or the first sentence is longer than 20 words.
